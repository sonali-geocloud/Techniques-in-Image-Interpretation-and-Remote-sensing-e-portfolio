M.Sc. Geoinformatics Semester I Integrated Reflection

My learning journey

Everything I learned in this classroom introduced me to concepts that were entirely new to me. Techniques in Image Interpretation and Remote Sensing has been one of the most interesting subjects in my academic journey; every class felt like a new adventure. It helped me understand how geographical information can be extracted from maps and imagery, interpreted, and transformed into useful spatial outputs.

From learning about Survey of India topographical maps to discovering where raw satellite data can be obtained, each topic added a new dimension to my understanding. I was introduced to image interpretation, band combinations, layer stacking, land-use and land-cover digitization, georeferencing, and the process of preparing clear map layouts. These lessons helped me see that a map is not simply an image: it is the outcome of careful data selection, processing, interpretation and presentation.

The topic I enjoyed most was satellite-image registration, especially image-to-image registration. Working with ground control points in QGIS showed me how important spatial alignment is when comparing images or bringing different sources together. Improving the placement of control points and examining residual errors also taught me that accuracy depends on careful decisions, not just on running a tool.

This subject has given me a foundation for the rest of my geospatial journey. The maps and practical outputs displayed here reflect the skills I began developing during the course, and I hope to build on them through more advanced remote-sensing analysis, GIS projects and research. The curiosity I felt in these classes is something I want to carry forward.
